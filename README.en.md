### Why Data and Banking/Finance Go Hand in Hand

A good metric without business context can lead to a poor decision; business intuition without reliable data remains fragile. My approach relies on a straightforward logic:

1. **Understand risk before modeling it:** 20 years of credit analysis and client advisory help pinpoint what truly matters in tax filings, cash flows, or client behavior—and avoid chasing false signals.
2. **Make data clear and actionable:** Turn raw or scattered data into reliable queries, clear KPIs, and decision-ready dashboards for business teams and stakeholders.
3. **Stay grounded in real-world operations:** Working as a financing broker keeps me in direct contact with credit files, lending criteria, and the economic realities of businesses and individuals.

### Flagship Project: Corporate Default Observatory

An analytical and credit decision-support tool designed to explore and assess corporate default risk at **1, 2, and 3 years** using financial and industry data.

- **Data Preparation & EDA:** Cleaning, structuring, and analyzing multi-year datasets (**Python / Pandas, SQL**).
- **Scoring & Survival Analysis:** Identifying key risk drivers and estimating time-to-event risk (**XGBoost Cox Model**).
- **Interactive Delivery:** Visual interface to explore risk indicators and scores (**Streamlit**).
